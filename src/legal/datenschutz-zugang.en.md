## 10. Access protection via invite links

This website is not public. It can only be reached via a **personal invite link** that our team committee sends to interested players. **In deviation from section 2**, the following applies:

**Access cookie.** When you open the site via your invite link, your browser stores a cookie named `tsv_zugang`. It only contains a random link identifier, the link's expiry date and a checksum, **no information about you as a person**. It is used only for this website and expires automatically together with the link (after 24 or 48 hours, depending on the link). The cookie is technically necessary so that you can keep viewing the site after your first visit. It is therefore set without separate consent (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

**Invite list.** So that the committee can keep track and revoke links if necessary, we store for each link: first name or initials of the invited person, who created the link, creation and expiry date, and whether the link was revoked. The data is stored at AWS in Frankfurt (Amazon DynamoDB), is accessible to the committee only, and is **deleted automatically 30 days after the link expires**. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is to make the site accessible to invited people only.

**Personal greeting.** When you visit the site, your browser uses the access cookie to retrieve the first name stored for your link, so that we can greet you by name on the home page. The name is only sent to the browser holding the valid cookie; it is not stored there or passed on to third parties. If the link has been revoked or has expired, no greeting is shown. The legal basis is also Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is to address invited players personally.
